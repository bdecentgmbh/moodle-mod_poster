# Poster — Student guide

A poster is a page your teacher has put together from blocks, for example texts, the latest
activity in your course, a calendar or a place for comments.

## Finding a poster

- **As a link on the course page:** open it like any other activity. The poster page shows its
  name, possibly a description, and its blocks in up to two columns. On a phone, the columns are
  shown one below the other.
- **Right on the course page:** some posters show their blocks directly on the course page, in the
  place of the activity, so there is nothing to open.

## Using the blocks

The blocks work as they do anywhere else in Moodle. Some show the same for everybody, such as a
text from your teacher; others show your own information, such as *Logged in user* or your course
progress.

What you write into a *Comments* block on a poster can be read by everybody who can see the poster.

## Completion

If your teacher has set it up, opening the poster page completes the activity.
